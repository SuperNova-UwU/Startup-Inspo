FAQ

Got questions? We've got answers.

What makes Velra different from Notion or Obsidian?

+

Traditional tools require you to organize everything manually — folders, tags, links. Velra uses AI to automatically understand, connect, and retrieve your ideas using deep semantic understanding. You just capture; Velra does the rest.

Is my data really private?

+

Yes. All data is end-to-end encrypted with zero-knowledge architecture. Your notes are encrypted on your device before reaching our servers. We literally cannot read them — and our architecture has been independently audited.

How much will Velra cost?

+

Free tier: unlimited captures, 1GB storage, core AI features. Premium (\$12/mo): unlimited storage, advanced AI, team spaces, priority support. Early access users get 3 months of Premium free.

What can I capture?

+

Text notes, web links, images, PDFs, screenshots, voice memos, bookmarks, tweets, Kindle highlights, podcast notes — virtually anything. We're adding new formats every month based on user feedback.

Does it work offline?

+

Yes. Capture, browse, and search offline — everything syncs seamlessly when you reconnect. Full offline AI-powered search is on our roadmap for Q2 2026.

Can I import from other tools?

+

Absolutely. One-click importers for Notion, Obsidian, Roam, Evernote, Apple Notes, Google Keep, and markdown files. Your knowledge graph starts building the moment you import.

How does Team Spaces work?

+

Share curated collections with your team while personal notes stay private. Think of it as a team wiki that builds itself — with AI connecting knowledge across all team members' shared captures.

What AI model does Velra use?

+

We use a custom fine-tuned model optimized specifically for knowledge retrieval and semantic understanding. It runs partially on-device for privacy, with cloud processing for advanced features — always encrypted.
